# The panel

Ten invented visitors. Keep who they are fixed between runs, so a change in what they say
reflects the app, not a new cast. **Last verdict** is the one line each gave on the latest
run: replace it each run, never append (git keeps the old ones).

Each entry: who they are, how they arrive, what they go for first, what wins them, what
loses them, how they talk.

---

## 1. Maddie, 19, Manchester (UK)
- **Arrives**: phone, from a group chat. Into F1 since *Drive to Survive*, knows the grid
  from 2019 on, almost nothing before. Fine with apps, but never reads instructions.
- **Goes for**: the most recent seasons, 2021 above all (Max vs Lewis), then her favourite
  driver.
- **Won by**: drama, a moment, something to screenshot. **Lost by**: spreadsheets, tapping
  Continue forty times, anything that looks like homework.
- **Voice**: quick, casual, "wait, what are the numbers?"
- **Last verdict** (2026-11-02): fun to see Max win again, but "it's just tables, I wanted
  to *see* it".

## 2. Rodrigo, 54, São Paulo (Brazil)
- **Arrives**: desktop. Saw Senna race at Interlagos. Knows the 1980s–90s deeply. Reads
  English slowly; Portuguese first.
- **Goes for**: 1994 and 1988, Senna, Brazilian drivers (Piquet, Fittipaldi, Barrichello).
- **Won by**: the what-if, Senna in another car, beating history. **Lost by**: Senna
  missing or underrated, English-only text, history that's wrong.
- **Voice**: warm, emotional, Portuguese words slipping in.
- **Last verdict** (2026-11-02): loved Senna in an Osella; "where is Ayrton in '94?"

## 3. Graham, 67, Northamptonshire (UK)
- **Arrives**: desktop. Has read Autosport since 1972 and keeps his own lap charts. Knows
  dropped scores, pre-qualifying and points systems by year. Careful, reads every word.
- **Goes for**: the About page first, then the 1950s–70s, the settings, the points rules.
- **Won by**: honesty about method, period rules shown on screen, correct detail. **Lost
  by**: rules applied but not explained, ratings with no rationale, factual slips.
- **Voice**: dry, precise, quietly approving or quietly devastating.
- **Last verdict** (2026-11-02): "unusually careful for a fan site" but wants a method page,
  and to see the period rules stated.

## 4. Lucía, 35, Valencia (Spain)
- **Arrives**: phone, a link from her partner, who watches F1. She doesn't. Ordinary
  smartphone user. Spanish first.
- **Goes for**: whatever the first screen tells her to do.
- **Won by**: knowing within ten seconds what this is and what to tap. **Lost by**: an
  unfinished look ("SOON"), jargon, walls of numbers.
- **Voice**: polite and short; leaves quietly.
- **Last verdict** (2026-11-02): "nice colours, but I didn't know what to do"; left within a
  minute.

## 5. Jakub, 29, Kraków (Poland)
- **Arrives**: desktop, from a dev forum. Front-end developer and occasional F1 viewer. Opens
  dev tools without thinking.
- **Goes for**: how it's built, the seed, sharing a run, the GitHub link.
- **Won by**: no server and no tracking, reproducible runs, links that keep state. **Lost
  by**: clever-but-unlabelled UI, state you can't share.
- **Voice**: terse, technical, half code review.
- **Last verdict** (2026-11-02): "pure in-browser sim, nice"; wants `?seed=` in the URL and
  labels on ▸ ⋯ ▾.

## 6. Ethan, 24, Brisbane (Australia)
- **Arrives**: desktop. Plays Football Manager and F1 Manager. Expects to be in control.
- **Goes for**: Game mode, then whatever lets him change things: Edit grid, eras.
- **Won by**: agency, long runs, "one more season". **Lost by**: watching without doing,
  features marked SOON.
- **Voice**: enthusiastic, gamer shorthand.
- **Last verdict** (2026-11-02): Edit grid is the hook; wants it up front and a "sim 10
  years" button.

## 7. Giuseppe, 72, Modena (Italy)
- **Arrives**: tablet with large text turned on; his grandson set it up. Ferrari fan for
  life, remembers Ascari as a boy. Not technical. Italian first.
- **Goes for**: the 1950s, Ferrari, Ascari, Fangio.
- **Won by**: the old names, readable text, plain words. **Lost by**: small grey text on
  black, icons and symbols he can't read, anything needing a gesture.
- **Voice**: slow, affectionate, a little lost.
- **Last verdict** (2026-11-02): delighted by 1952 Ferrari, but "the writing is too small,
  and what is this little arrow?"

## 8. Haruto, 31, Tokyo (Japan)
- **Arrives**: phone. Tsunoda and Honda fan, follows every session. Reads English fine.
- **Goes for**: 2021 onwards, Tsunoda's rating, Honda engine years.
- **Won by**: being able to argue with a rating, liveries done right. **Lost by**: his driver
  rated low with no way to change it.
- **Voice**: polite, then firm about Yuki.
- **Last verdict** (2026-11-02): "68 is too low!" but the team colours are "much better than
  I expected".

## 9. Valentina, 27, Guadalajara (Mexico)
- **Arrives**: phone, from a TikTok. Checo Pérez fan, makes F1 content for Instagram and

- **Goes for**: Pérez's seasons (2021–2024), the Share button, anything worth posting.
- **Won by**: a good-looking card or image she can post, a result that makes a story.

- **Voice**: fast, emoji-adjacent, thinks in captions.
- **Last verdict**: not on the first panel; no verdict yet.

## 10. Priya, 38, Bengaluru (India)
- **Arrives**: desktop, keyboard and a screen reader (NVDA); she has low vision. Software
  tester by trade. Watches F1 on Sundays with her husband.
- **Goes for**: the main path end to end by keyboard: setup, a season, the standings.
- **Won by**: every control named, sensible focus order, dialogs that trap and release
  focus, tables that read as tables. **Lost by**: unnamed buttons, stray symbols read
  aloud, focus lost after a dialog, colour as the only signal.
- **Voice**: methodical, files bugs in her head as she goes.
- **Last verdict**: not on the first panel; no verdict yet.
