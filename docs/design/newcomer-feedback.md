# Newcomer feedback

What the invented visitor panel (`.claude/skills/persona-panel/`) found, as changes ranked by
value for effort. Nothing here is agreed: PROPOSED items wait for the author's yes, OPEN ones
go against a choice the author may have made on purpose. A built item is deleted; a new
panel run re-ranks the rest. Numbers in brackets are panel members (`personas.md`).

## The first two minutes (small)

- PROPOSED **Say what the numbers are, where they are**: a one-line key over the grid
  (car rating, driver ability, out of 100) and a tooltip on each badge. [1 3 4 8]
- PROPOSED **A cleaner home menu**: drop the "Mode / Status" header, show the two ready modes
  as clear buttons, fold the SOON ones into one "coming later" line. [4 6]
- OPEN **A one-tap start**: a row of well-known seasons, or open the picker on the latest
  decade rather than the fifties. Which seasons, and whether the fifties are the front
  door on purpose, is the author's call. [1 2]
- PROPOSED **Words, not glyphs**: "More" for ⋯, a label or tooltip on ▸ ▾ ▶ ›, "Seed"
  for the bare "Run 123456" (or hide it). [4 7 5]
- PROPOSED **Simulate whole season on a phone**, not only behind ⋯; and check the sticky
  bars on a real phone (`pnpm tunnel`), since full-page captures float them over rows. [1]
- OPEN **Readable by default**: larger smallest text in phone tables; open in the device's
  light or dark setting (`prefers-color-scheme`) instead of always dark. [7 10]

## Worth sharing (moderate)

- PROPOSED **The what-ifs up front**: two or three scenario cards on the home page that start
  in one tap, and Edit grid offered there. [6 2 9]
- PROPOSED **Race highlights**: two or three lines per race derived from the results
  (biggest climber, pole to win, a front-runner out, a first win). Display only. [1 9]
- OPEN **A season's story**: when the title was clinched, who led when, first wins. A "vs
  real life" line needs real results in the app, which brings in the Jolpica licence. [1 3]
- PROPOSED **A champion's moment**: the title cards made to feel like a win. [1 9]
- PROPOSED **Show the period rules**: say "best N results count" in the points setting and
  standings, and mark the dropped scores. [3]
- PROPOSED **Explain the ratings**: a sentence per attribute on the driver card and a "how
  ratings work" section on About; the words are the author's. [3 8]
- OPEN **Missing drivers** (Senna in 1994): a note on the grid that lineups are the
  season's main pairing, or a way to add him from Edit grid. Whether he belongs in the
  1994 data is the author's decision. [2]

## Bigger (mostly on the roadmap already)

- PROPOSED **The bug report link** from *Publish it*: real visitors are the feedback that
  counts.
- PROPOSED **A seed in the URL** (`?seed=`), far smaller than the full Share link. [5 9]
- PROPOSED **Jump to… easier to find** in eras, where today it shows only wide or behind ⋯. [6]
- OPEN **Translations** (Portuguese, Spanish, Italian): once the wording settles, or every
  string is translated twice. [2 4 7 9]
- OPEN **Watching a race unfold**: a stepped reveal, or the parade following the race
  order. It goes against the results-only race model, so a direction question first. [1 6]
- Already roadmap: the ratings editor [3 8], the driver market and Game mode [6].
