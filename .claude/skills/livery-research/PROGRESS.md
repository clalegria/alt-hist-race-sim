# Livery research progress

Teams whose eras in `packages/data/src/teams.json` the author has signed off. Skip them
unless asked; add a team here when the author approves it. Every team not listed still
carries its original, unresearched colours, so treat its values as suspect when it
clashes with a team under review.

## Researched with this skill

- `ags`: whole history 1986-1991 (1991 era runs on). Thin evidence: 1986-87 (museum cars
  only), 1990 (demo cars only); 1989's yellow rests on one demo car.
- `alfa_romeo` (the works team, not `sauber`): 1980-85. 1983 rests on museum cars only;
  1950-79 keeps its original colours.
- `arrows`: whole history 1978-2002.
- `ats` (the 1977-84 German team, not `ats_63`): whole history, one yellow era. 1981 has
  no photo.
- `brabham`: whole history 1962-1991. 1972 rests on museum cars only; 1992 (pink/blue
  BT60B) needs its own era if that season is added.
- `caterham` (Lotus Racing + Caterham, 2010-2014): whole history. 2013 rests on launch
  studio renders only.
- `coloni`: whole history 1987-1991; 1991 is the author's own. 1987 has no photo (carries
  1988's yellow); 1988 rests on demo cars; 1989-90 on one illustration each. 1990 follows
  the Subaru C3B (white, red, green), not the yellow Ford C3C of the second half.
- `dallara` (Dallara + the 1993 BMS Lola): whole history 1988-1993. 1993 is half red,
  half white; red taken as primary.
- `eurobrun`: whole history 1988-1990. 1989 (orange) rests on demo and museum cars only;
  1990 (silver) on one author-supplied photo.
- `ferrari`: whole history 1950-2026; solid red but for white-stripe eras (1975-78,
  2015-18, 2025+) and 2024's yellow/white. Pre-1970 is the author's own (solid red).
  Single-source: 1978 (one colour demo); 2021 and 2023 have no photo.
- `fittipaldi`: whole history 1975-1982. 1975 rests on one museum car; 1980-81 on demo cars
  only; 1979 and 1982 have no photo (each carries its era). 1977-79 shares Renault's
  yellow, told apart by the stripes.
- `fondmetal` (1991-1992, not `osella`): whole history, one era. It is the author's own
  1991 era, run on through 1992 to match a 1992 Monaco photo.
- `forti`: whole history 1995-1996, one yellow era. 1996's late Shannon-era livery is
  not modelled.
- `haas` (the 2016+ team, not `haas_lola`): whole history; 2025-26 is one era.
- `haas_lola` (1985-1986, not `haas`): whole history, one era. The Beatrice red is leaned
  crimson to clear Ferrari; told from Zakspeed by the blue stripe.
- `hrt`: whole history 2010-2012. 2011's red is split between two photos; 2012's red and
  gold are read by eye.
- `larrousse`: whole history 1987-1994. 1987-88 rests on demo cars only; 1991 has no photo
  (carries the 1989-93 navy look).
- `life`: whole history 1990, plain deep red, darker than Ferrari's by the author's choice.
- `ligier` (Ligier + Prost, 1976-2001): whole history. Single-source: 1976-78 sky blue,
  2001 red stripe.
- `lotus` (Team Lotus 1958-1994 only, not `toleman` or `caterham`): whole history. 1991
  is the author's own; 1981 kept as Essex.
- `march` (March + Leyton House, 1970-1992): whole history. Single-source: 1973 (orange-red,
  one museum car), 1992 (turquoise, one blurry photo); 1987 and 1989 have no photo.
  1977 follows Ribeiro's Hollywood car, not Scheckter's Rothmans one.
- `mclaren`: 1966-73. 1974-96 (Marlboro) and 1997+ are the author's own eras, kept.
- `minardi`: 1985-2005; 2006+ is the author's own. No photo: 1988, 1992, 2002 (each
  carries the season before). Thin evidence: 1991 and 1997 (museum cars only), 2000
  (scale models only). 1985-88 and 1995-96 are half and half; yellow and navy taken as
  primary.
- `shadow`: whole history 1973-1980. Thin evidence: 1977 (demo cars only), 1980 (demo cars
  only), 1975 yellow (one museum car). 1979 follows de Angelis's black Villiger car, not
  Lammers's navy Samson one.
- `simtek`: whole history 1994-1995. 1994 is half violet, half black; violet taken as
  primary. 1995 rests on one period photo (the rest are demo cars).
- `spirit`: whole history 1983-1984. 1983 (blue Virginiana car) rests on one museum car;
  1984's plain white is a guess from a historic demo car in a later livery.
- `stewart` (Stewart + Jaguar): 1997-2004; 2005+ (Red Bull) is the author's own. Thin
  evidence: 1997-98 (museum cars only), 2000 (no photo; carries the R2-R5 look back).
- `super_aguri`: whole history 2006-2008, one era. Stripes run black then red to tell it
  from Toyota's red and navy; the red shade is unconfirmed.
- `theodore`: whole history 1981-1983. 1981-82 white rests on black-and-white photos only;
  its red and black accents are a guess. 1983's navy rests on one small period scan.
- `toyota`: whole history 2002-2009, one era.
- `onyx`: whole history 1989-1990. 1990 (lime) rests on one preserved Monteverdi car; 1989's
  pink shade is a guess from pinstripes.
- `osella` (1980-1990, not `fondmetal`): whole history. 1989-90 carry 1988 over with no
  photo; 1982 rests on one illustration.
- `pacific`: whole history 1994-1995. 1995's blue is nudged off Jordan's (ΔE 14, told
  apart by the green stripe).
- `rial`: whole history 1988-1989, one era. Rests on demo cars from one 2024 meeting; the
  blue is lightened off Onyx's.
- `ram`: whole history 1983-1985. 1983 (white) rests on one museum car; 1984 has no photo
  (carries 1985's Skoal Bandit green).
- `tyrrell` (Tyrrell → BAR → Honda → Brawn → Mercedes): whole history 1970-2026. Thin
  evidence: 1987 (one illustration), 1990 (no photo), 1997 (a scale model only).
- `virgin` (Virgin → Marussia → Manor): whole history 2010-2016. 2014 is merged with
  2015 (red with white and black).
- `williams`: 1975-2026. 1971-72 keeps placeholder colours until a photo turns up.
- `zakspeed`: whole history 1985-1989, one era. 1987 rests on one illustration.
