The share image

Two cards for each mode. The player picks one, or both. The team colours now run only through a car band of their own, so a livery can never hide a word or a year. Every result is invented; the colours are the authored liveries.

Colours

  • The fix: the sash only runs inside a ruled band across the card that holds the car and nothing else. Every word, bar and badge sits on graphite, so this works for any livery.
  • On graphite, a dark livery (a black Mercedes, a dark green Lotus) still shows, because its bars and badges carry the hairline the app gives every badge.

Rules

  • Ties on titles: whoever won first comes first.
  • The other champions: up to ten rows, the rest in one sentence.
  • Lists on the second cards: up to 20 rows; an era shorter than 20 seasons lists them all.
