# Graphic marks

Three drawn marks added to the timing-tower look (`docs/style/timing-tower.md`), all
built. Like that file, this one is kept as a reference for a later tweak, not a plan.

**The mockup is `docs/style/graphic-marks-mockup.html`**: open it in a browser. Its
sections 4, 5 and 7 are the marks below as first drawn; the app has moved on from them
where noted. Sections 1, 2, 3 and 8 (gap bars, places gained, form strip, pit board) were
not chosen and stay only as drawn examples.

## What holds for all three

- **No new hue and no new voice.** Teal means "won the thing on screen" and is the only
  colour a mark adds; team colours are drawn as their badge draws them.
- **Every figure is derived on read** from result rows, never kept.

## The marks

- **Title count** (`TitleCount`; mockup 4): a teal trophy after a champion's name, with a
  count from two titles, nothing for none. A trophy rather than the mockup's stars, which
  clashed with the home star. On the roll of honour and the driver and team pages.
- **Livery barcode** (`LiveryBarcode`; mockup 5): one stripe per season in that season's
  livery, a teal caret on the year the run has reached. Under a team page's heading, and
  later a driver's too, where a stripe names its team.
- **Podium** (`Podium`; mockup 7): the race page's top three on stepped blocks in 2-1-3
  order, above a classification that still lists them. The blocks are the position block
  grown, with the gap and points on one line; on a phone the large car is shrunk to fit a
  third of the screen.
